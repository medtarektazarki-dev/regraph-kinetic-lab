## Services

### Brand Motion Film

A short motion-led film that introduces a brand, campaign, or point of view. Includes creative direction, storyboard, motion design, sound direction, and agreed delivery formats.

### Product Launch Video

A product-focused video for a launch, feature announcement, or campaign. The scope can include product storytelling, interface or product animation, kinetic typography, and social cutdowns.

### Social Video Package

A planned set of platform-ready edits from one approved creative direction. Aspect ratios, durations, captions, and the number of versions are defined in the quote.

### Motion Direction & Storyboard

For teams producing in-house: reference analysis, visual direction, timed storyboard, typography and transition notes, plus a production handoff. This is a standalone planning service and does not include a finished video unless agreed.

## How a project works

1. **Send the brief** — describe the brand, audience, objective, deadline, platforms, and assets.
2. **Get a scoped proposal** — receive the deliverables, schedule, revision rounds, usage terms, and price in writing.
3. **Approve the direction** — agree on the concept and storyboard before full production.
4. **Review and deliver** — provide feedback within the agreed rounds, then receive the final exports and agreed project files.

## Scope and pricing

Every quote depends on the length, complexity, number of versions, supplied assets, schedule, and licensing needs. Contact Regraph Studio for a tailored quote. No payment is due and no work is booked until the scope, price, and schedule are agreed in writing.

Unless the proposal says otherwise, third-party assets, paid stock, voice talent, music licensing, rush work, extra versions, and additional revision rounds are scoped separately. Ownership and usage rights for the final deliverables are stated in the proposal.
