# Delivery specifications

Use the user's requested output settings first. If absent, infer a reasonable target from destination and ask only when different targets would require materially different framing or duration.

Before rendering, confirm composition dimensions, frame rate, duration, codec/container, audio requirements, and destination. Keep source composition dimensions distinct from platform UI-safe areas. Verify current platform guidance when it may have changed, and label paid-ad safe zones separately from organic content recommendations.

Use a high-quality intermediate or project-native render while iterating; encode the final requested container only after review. Avoid extra formats and resolutions unless requested.
