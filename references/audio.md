# Audio direction

- Determine whether the video needs music, voice, sound design, or silence from the brief.
- Use only user-provided audio or a track with rights for the intended use. Do not assume a reference's music may be reused.
- If syncing to music, measure the track's beat grid and place major transitions intentionally. Do not infer exact BPM from visuals alone.
- Keep voice intelligible over music. Use restrained effects with clear visual causes; avoid adding a whoosh to every movement.
- Check loudness, clipping, channel layout, and sync in the final encoded file when tools permit. State when audio was not reviewed.
